Quickdot is a GPLv3 fork of Quik (which continues QKSMS). It is not the official Quik app, not a Nothing product, and not affiliated with Nothing or the Quik project.

Quickdot brings a sharp, minimal monochrome dot-matrix style to everyday texting: square marks, Doto and Space Mono type, and a red accent on the free Google Play build. SMS and MMS work as you expect—scheduled messages, backup, blocking, attachments, voice notes, and the rest of the Quik feature set.

<b>Free on Google Play</b>
• Full Quickdot interface with the red accent everywhere
• Send and receive messages without limits

<b>Quickdot Pro</b> (one-time purchase on Google Play)
• Custom accent colour across the app
• Twelve preset accents plus a #RRGGBB hex picker with a contrast check
• Per-conversation accents (bubbles, Send button, rules, avatars, notifications)
• Reply with a quote to the message you are answering

<b>FOSS build</b>
A separate FOSS flavour ships without Google Play billing and includes every Pro feature.

Messaging stays free. Pro only unlocks personalisation and reply quotes.
